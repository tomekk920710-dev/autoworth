AutoWorth V1.6.0

Economics model correction:
- Added Human review rate (%) as a separate assumption from AI error rate (%).
- Human review labor cost = monthly tasks × review rate × review minutes per reviewed task × labor cost/hour ÷ 60.
- AI error/rework allowance remains separate and is stress-tested at 1%, 3%, 5%, and 10%.
- Default review rate is 100% to avoid optimistic savings assumptions.
- Existing saved reports without reviewRate preserve their legacy review-rate assumption when reopened.

Example: 500 tasks/month, 3 review minutes, $30/hour:
- At 100% review rate: human review labor = $750/month.
- At 3% review rate: human review labor = $22.50/month.
This makes the operational assumption explicit instead of deriving it from AI error rate.
